🛠️ About FreeTools

Simple & Free Online Tools

Welcome to FreeTools

FreeTools is a simple online platform that provides useful tools for everyday calculations and tasks.

Our goal is to make common online tools easy to use, fast, and accessible to everyone.

Our Tools

FreeTools currently provides tools such as an Age Calculator, Percentage Calculator, EMI Calculator, Word Counter, BMI Calculator, and Discount Calculator.

Why FreeTools?

We aim to provide simple tools with an easy-to-understand interface without requiring users to create an account.
