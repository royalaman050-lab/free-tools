How to Use FreeTools

FreeTools provides simple online tools for common calculations and everyday tasks. Choose a tool, enter the required information, and select the calculation button to see the result.

Age Calculator

The Age Calculator uses your date of birth to calculate your current age in completed years.

Percentage Calculator

The Percentage Calculator helps you find a percentage of a number. Enter the number and percentage value to calculate the result.

EMI Calculator

The EMI Calculator estimates a monthly loan payment using the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan period in months.

Word Counter

The Word Counter counts the words in text that you type or paste into the text box. It can be useful for checking the length of written content.

BMI Calculator

The BMI Calculator calculates Body Mass Index using your weight in kilograms and height in centimetres. BMI is a general screening measurement and is not a medical diagnosis.

Discount Calculator

The Discount Calculator calculates the amount saved and the final price after applying a percentage discount to an original price.
